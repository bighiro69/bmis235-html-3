Cliffjumping

For this page I wanted to talk about hitting the top jump at Box Canyon, which is about 65 feet. I chose to include this because not only was that jump the one that made me not scared to commit to many other things in the future, but the trek down is so heinous that it discourages most people from ever even coming to the spot.

When I said I wanted to backflip 60 feet, this is likely the spot I want to do it at. It is roughly 30 minutes from home, and it is a beautiful area with lots of great places to film, as long as a decent runup. However, getting to this spot is pretty challenging as park rangers have started to enforce people coming in and out of here, due to injuries and noise complaints. The second video I added was huge for me because that day I showed up to that spot having ruled out the top cliff, but after stepping up 5 feet at a time starting from 30, I knew that I could do it. This was a huge mental buff for me, and since this day I feel like I have been able to commit to everything with a little more ease.
