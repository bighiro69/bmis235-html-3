The first stunt I thought about when trying to think of what to write about was flipping my bike. This trick had a lot of mental struggle as it is very hard to convince yourself that you both can and should be upside down on a mountain bike during any given time.

This trick is easy in terms of skill, as all you need to do is lean back and adjust in the air. With that being said, it is very hard to nonchalantly do those things so one thing I struggled alot with was not pulling so hard as to make the bike hit my body, as you can see happening in the landing clip. Now that I'm better at flipping though, I have been wanting to retry this trick.
