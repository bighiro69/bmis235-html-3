Cliff Climbing

This page is dedicated to a venture I had this summer, where I had never felt closer to dying. This was in La Jolla, and my friends and I climbed a bluff that looked reachable. Although this wasn't fully legal, it was pretty tame so I decided it was alright to add.

The reason this one was scary was because we managed to get search and rescue called on us while going up it. At the top, it was a 30 degree slope downward into an easily 80+ foot drop onto a sand beach, so you could see death pretty easily. While we were sitting up there, a search and rescue helicopter appeared and passed us for the first time, which was a pretty cool sight until I realized that we had a heli on us and we were stuck on a cliff. Looking forwawrd all I could see was the way down, and the way to escape was kind of to the side, which was very treacherous. The helicopter made 2 more passes during which we had to hide in bushes, and they also sent 2 rangers to find us who we saw coming down the mountain towards our general location once we got off the bluff. Escaping this was super exhilerating, as well as the feeling of conquering the fear of death to do so. After checking flight radar the whole way home, the rush we got after getting there was insane. I wish I could include more activities, but I also won't snitch on myself. This website is a display of random things I could talk about, and I enjoyed making it.
